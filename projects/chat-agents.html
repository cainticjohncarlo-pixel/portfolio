● Featured sample work AI sales bot, live conversation

An always-on AI sales assistant that runs the full inbound motion.

Wired into the CRM and the live calendar, the bot handles every incoming lead the moment it arrives, no waiting on a human to open Slack. It runs four jobs end-to-end and only escalates when human judgment is actually needed.

01 · Respond

Answers inbound leads instantly, 24/7

Web chat, SMS, and WhatsApp all funnel into one assistant. A new lead gets a personal reply in seconds, regardless of time zone or office hours.

02 · Qualify

Runs a structured discovery flow

Trained on your real ICP, offer, and objection library. It scores fit and intent, then tags the lead in the CRM so the pipeline sorts itself.

03 · Book

Handles appointments end-to-end

Pulls live calendar availability, offers slots the lead can pick, confirms the booking, and drops the meeting on the calendar with reminders wired in.

04 · Close

Closes the deal or hands off cleanly

Sends the proposal or checkout link when the lead is ready, or hands off to a rep with the full conversation, tags, and next-step context attached.
