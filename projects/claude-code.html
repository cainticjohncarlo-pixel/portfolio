Jarvis Agent, a personal AI operator.

Jarvis is a custom AI assistant built end-to-end with Claude Code. It listens to natural-language requests, decides which tools to call, executes the work across your stack, and reports back, replacing what used to take five tabs and ten manual steps with a single conversation.

  • Tool-calling across CRM, calendar, email, and web search
  • Persistent memory across sessions and tasks
  • Planning loop that breaks complex requests into executable steps
  • Human-in-the-loop handoff when judgement is needed
