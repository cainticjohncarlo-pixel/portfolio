Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how John Carlo Caintic ("I," "me," or "we") collects, uses, and protects information about visitors to johncarlo-portfolio.vercel.app and clients of the services described on this website ("Services").

1. Information I collect

I collect the minimum information needed to deliver Services and respond to inquiries.

  • Information you provide directly, your name, email address, company, phone number, and any details you submit through the booking form, contact form, or in email correspondence.
  • Booking and meeting data, when you book a discovery call through the embedded calendar, your scheduling information is processed by GoHighLevel (the calendar provider) and shared with me to confirm the meeting.
  • Project information, during a paid engagement, I may receive credentials, business data, and operational details necessary to build the agreed deliverables. This data is treated as confidential.
  • Usage data, basic aggregate analytics (page views, country, referrer) collected by Vercel, the hosting provider, to understand site performance. No personal profiling.

2. How I use information

Information you provide is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule discovery calls.
  • Deliver the Services agreed in a written engagement.
  • Send project updates, invoices, and engagement-related communication.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

I do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-party services

This website and my Services rely on a small number of trusted third-party providers, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel, hosting and basic analytics.
  • GoHighLevel, discovery call scheduling.
  • Google Workspace, email correspondence and document sharing.
  • Project tools (during engagement), n8n, Make, Zapier, OpenAI, Anthropic, and similar platforms used to deliver Services. Specific tools are disclosed in the engagement scope.

4. Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies, only those required for basic site functionality and aggregate analytics. No third-party advertising cookies are placed by this site.

5. Data security

I take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted transit (HTTPS), access controls on shared client data, and prompt deletion of data no longer needed.

6. Data retention

I retain inquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact. Engagement data is retained for the duration of the project plus 12 months for reference, after which it is deleted unless a longer retention is contractually agreed or legally required.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR for EU residents, CCPA for California residents), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the personal information I hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email cainticjohncarlo@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

This website and my Services are not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact me at cainticjohncarlo@gmail.com.
