Kridet Solutions: AI lead qualification agent

An AI agent that answers Instagram, Facebook and SMS leads, routes them to the right service, qualifies them and books the call.

Kridet Solutions offers credit restoration and business funding. Leads arrive from Instagram and Facebook comments, DMs and SMS, and each one needs a fast reply, the right questions for the right service, and a booked call, without the team answering every message by hand.

ManyChat entry→Closebot AI agent→GoHighLevel CRM→Calendar booking→Pre-call video

ManyChat catches comment keywords and DMs. The Closebot agent runs the conversation. GoHighLevel holds the fields, tags, calendar and workflows.

  • A discover-and-route step splits funding leads from credit restoration leads.
  • Each path has its own qualification flow, and every answer is written to GoHighLevel custom fields.
  • Proof branches handle common questions on funding, collections, charge-offs and student loans.
  • Booking offers live calendar slots, then sends a pre-call video and confirms it was watched.
  • Handover paths pass upset or complex conversations to a person, and tag rules keep the agent away from existing clients.

Published and running on the client's account since July 2026. Lead and booking numbers belong to the client and are not published here.

My contribution

Designed the conversation flow, built the agent, and built the GoHighLevel side: fields, tags, calendar and workflows.

n8n · Al's Barber Shop: Vapi + GoHighLevel CRM

Real build. Each Vapi tool is its own n8n webhook that talks to GoHighLevel.

Demo buildVoice AILocal service business

AI voice receptionist with live booking

A phone agent that recognises returning callers and books, cancels or looks up appointments in the CRM while the caller is still on the line.

Small service businesses miss calls while they are with a customer, and every missed call is a booking that goes elsewhere. The receptionist has to answer every call, know who is calling, and change the real calendar, not just take a message.

Caller→Vapi assistant→Tool call→n8n webhook→GoHighLevel API→Spoken reply

Deepgram transcribes, GPT-4.1 reasons and decides which tool to call, and an ElevenLabs voice speaks the answer.

  • Four tools, each its own n8n webhook: lookup caller, book, cancel and get appointments.
  • The caller lookup runs before the first word, so returning callers are greeted by name.
  • Booking finds or creates the contact, then creates the appointment on the GoHighLevel calendar.
  • Cancel checks that the contact and the appointment exist, and returns a clear spoken answer for every branch.

campground-platform.vercel.app

Live app, running in demo mode. Send a test reservation and watch each step run.

Engineering demoIntegration pipelineHospitality

Outpost: reservation-to-guest pipeline

One reservation webhook that stores the guest, updates loyalty, syncs the CRM and sends the welcome email and text, in one pass.

A campground or RV park takes bookings from several systems. Each booking should land in one guest record, trigger the welcome messages and update loyalty history, without staff copying data between tools.

Reservation webhook→Mapping layer→Supabase→Loyalty→GoHighLevel→Email + SMS

Supabase is the source of truth. GoHighLevel is the activation layer for messaging.

  • A vendor-agnostic mapping layer turns any booking system's payload into one guest format.
  • Each step runs in isolation and is idempotent, so a failed step can re-run without duplicates.
  • Every integration has a demo mode, so the whole pipeline runs safely with no credentials set.
  • A test console sends a reservation and shows the event log step by step.
Next.js 14TypeScriptTailwindSupabaseGoHighLevelVercel

A live engineering demo. It runs in demo mode, so no real guest data is stored or sent. Each integration switches to live once its credentials are added.

My contribution

Designed the architecture and built the whole app: webhook, mapping layer, pipeline, schema and test console.

Today I build those systems end to end: AI agents and voice assistants, workflow automation, API and CRM integrations, and the custom applications around them. I start from how the business actually runs, design the flow before I build anything, and test the edge cases, because that is where most automations break.
